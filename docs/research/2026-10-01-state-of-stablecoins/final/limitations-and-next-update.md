## Funding-source stopping decision

The targeted follow-up traced the Fed note's cited literature to the BIS monetary-policy/MMF
study (SS-008) and New York Fed flight-to-safety study (SS-009), and checked the BIS
macroeconomic model (SS-015). The captured publisher abstracts describe aggregate shock
responses, within-stablecoin flows and calibrated model scenarios respectively. They do not
establish purchaser origin-asset funding shares. Full-paper datasets were not independently
reanalysed, so this is not a claim that such evidence cannot exist.

The quantitative decomposition remains UNRESOLVED. Collection is now bounded: no
market-wide net new Treasury-demand or debt-relief number will be asserted. Retain the
qualitative mechanism and attributed yield evidence. A later source with actual origin-asset
data can reopen this gap. This is enough to finalise a qualified report, subject to the
independent continuation review and reconciliation; it is not enough to prove fiscal relief.

## Narratives allowed, not unsupported facts

The operator approved retaining inconclusive narratives with clear citations or
footnotes. Each narrative must name its evidence, competing explanation and missing
test. UNRESOLVED remains explicit.

- Treasury narrative: issuer growth can redirect short-term safe-asset demand, but
  gross Treasury exposure does not measure incremental demand or fiscal relief.
  Conditional funding mechanisms and estimated yield effects are distinct evidence.
- Distribution narrative: partner-oriented reserve earnings and an equity opportunity
  may alter incentives. Existing Circle distribution economics make novelty and
  superiority separate questions. Neither misallocation nor superior adoption is proved.
- RWA narrative: much reported tokenized value does not move publicly each week.
  Represented assets may deliberately function as ledgers, so low transfers are not
  evidence of failure, no yield or no off-chain servicing.
- x402 narrative: a payment mechanism is in use, but gross rail volume does not yet
  establish broad goods-and-services commerce. No commerce residual is asserted.

## Preserved limitations and corrections

R3-01 documented an extractor that swallowed real text around literal less-than
signs. The name-anchored replacement and legacy normalizer remain reproducible.
This continuation does not claim independent re-measurement of that historical fix.

Researcher-authored digests elsewhere in the upstream corpus cannot verify their
own quotes. SS-085 previously cited a derived digest; it now cites the origin HTML payload.
The derived digest is hashed as a companion, not treated as an origin quote. Known extraction
headers are rejected by the passage verifier; that check does not prove all provenance. Source
quotations preserve their typography; authored prose uses neither em nor en dashes.

The original Kansas City Fed HTML, secondary RWA wording and independent review
files are preserved. SS-071's count interpretation and SS-063's unqualified approval
reading are explicitly superseded in the continuation disposition, not silently
rewritten as if the earlier review had reached the new conclusions.

Other exclusions remain: the invented 95-to-99 percent non-payment share, unsupported
Circle price-fall claim for the earlier event, uncaptured OUSD holder count, unaligned market-cap range,
unsourced x402 floor and direct OUSD title/instruction rights. BUIDL figures remain
attributed, dated secondary reports rather than a contemporaneous verified range.
The separate CNBC July share-price rise is retained as attributed secondary reporting (SS-106),
not independent price verification or proof about a different announcement.

   Treasury-demand conclusion can be made. Seek a KC Fed publisher reconciliation
   separately; fixing that example alone does not close the funding question.
2. Obtain comparable Open Standard contract terms and realized payouts before
   calling its economics more generous or structurally superior.
3. Obtain the full RWA report and dataset, then test economic utility separately
   from weekly transfers. The denominator correction is already made.

   Do not subtract incompatible firm totals to invent it.
5. Seek BUIDL-specific dated disclosure, current OCC authorization records and
   authoritative GENIUS final-rule records. Keep historical and current status apart.
6. Build OUSD partner-use observations and align market-cap definitions only when
   the necessary source observations can be obtained.
