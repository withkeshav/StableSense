# Methodology

Every metric on agent economy is built from public on-chain activity — here's exactly how.

 [Back to home](https://agenteconomy.to/)

From block to number

Four steps, run continuously — from public chain data to one clean feed.

1. 01


   ### Pull from the chain



   Agent-payment events are sourced from public on-chain data — through Dune and direct RPC indexing — across every tracked network.

2. 02


   ### Decode & normalize



   Raw events become one consistent shape — protocol, chain, agent, amount, timestamp — and are deduplicated across sources.

3. 03


   ### Aggregate



   Totals, daily and monthly series, and market share are computed from those events — not hand-entered or adjusted.

4. 04


   ### Publish



   Everything lands in a single schema-stable, MCP-native data.json that refreshes continuously. One source, versioned and citable.
