# Research publication checklist

Research completion, owner acceptance, public release and site deployment are
separate decisions. This checklist makes their evidence visible; it does not
assign an automatic publication verdict.

## Research revision

- The brief, scope and exclusions are frozen with the exact revision.
- Each material claim has a dated source, usable locator and preserved context.
- Exact capture bytes and derived extractions are distinguished and hashed.
- Every numerical claim was recomputed from its declared inputs with actual output.
- Incompatible dates, units, populations and denominators remain separate.
- Independent findings have source-checked dispositions, including unresolved ones.
- Central unresolved claims are visible and not disguised as confirmed conclusions.
- Dependencies and claim-specific update triggers are recorded.
- The owner has separately recorded acceptance of this research revision.

## Proposed public release

- The owner approved this exact revision for the named public surface.
- Public citations identify the original publisher and relevant source context;
  private filesystem paths are not the only citations.
- Raw captures, transcripts, drafts, internal plans, legal drafts and review notes
  remain outside the public repository. Agent instructions and configuration are
  not public publication material.
- The private retention copy and remote commit were checked before any removal.
- Selected source passages and fixtures have checked reuse rights.
- No credentials, private contacts or consent-dependent identities are included.
- Unknowns, exclusions and as-of dates are visible wherever the claim appears.
- Captions, chart data, figures and headline text use the same measured quantities.
- The license and contribution credit are preserved without implying endorsement.
- Local links and media resolve; no authored em or en dashes were introduced.
- Test and build commands were run, with their actual outputs recorded separately
  from factual verification.

## Release and subsequent update

- A repository release is verified by matching local and remote commits and the
  exact target files, not only by a successful push.
- Site updates and deployment require their own authorization and verification.
- If deployed, check the exact live revision and its public figures and captions.
- Keep prior evidence versions and corrections; do not rewrite findings silently.
- A failed retrieval or absent measurement remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED.
- Benchmark claims name the fixture version, answer-key review and measured runs.
  Application tests and illustrative arithmetic are not benchmark measurements.

Current-tree removal is not confidentiality: earlier public commits and downloaded
copies can still contain working records. History rewriting is a separate decision.
